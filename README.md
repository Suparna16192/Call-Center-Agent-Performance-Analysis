# Call-Center-Agent-Performance-Analysis
This project focuses on analyzing call center data to evaluate operational performance, customer service efficiency, and overall call-handling trends. The analysis examines key metrics such as total call volume, answer rate, abandoned calls, average call duration, customer satisfaction, and agent performance.


By identifying call patterns, peak call periods, high-performing areas, and potential service gaps, the dashboard helps stakeholders understand how effectively the call center is operating. The insights generated can support data-driven decisions to improve response times, optimize staffing, reduce call abandonment, and enhance the overall customer experience.

## Objective

The objective is to measure customer service performance by evaluating key metrics such as:

Total Call Volume
Answer Rate
Resolution Rate
Agent Performance
Customer Satisfaction
Average Talk Duration
Speed of Answer
Call Trends by Topic, Date and Hour

The project demonstrates how SQL can be used to extract business insights from operational datasets before creating interactive dashboards in Power BI.

## Dataset
Analyzed 5,000 call center records using SQL. The dateset contains the following columns : Call_Id,	Agent,	Date,	Time,	Topic,	Answered_(Y/N),	Resolved,	Speed_of_answer_in_seconds,	AvgTalkDuration and	Satisfaction_rating.

## Insights

1.The overall call answer rate was 81.08%, meaning that 18.92% of incoming calls were not answered.Review unanswered calls by hour and increase agent availability during peak periods. Call-back options or improved call-routing processes may also help reduce missed calls. 

2.The call center operated between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Call volume varied throughout the day, ranging from approximately 14 to 594 calls, with the highest call volume of 594 calls occurring around 1:00 PM.Schedule additional agents or stagger breaks around the peak period to maintain service levels and reduce customer wait times. 

3.The overall call resolution rate was 72.92%, indicating that approximately 27.08% of calls were not resolved during the analyzed interaction.Technical Support had the lowest resolution rate at 72.23%. Review common unresolved technical issues and improve troubleshooting guides, technical training, or access to specialist support. 

4.Streaming received the highest average customer satisfaction rating at 2.82, while Technical Support received the lowest rating at 2.70. Technical Support may require further attention because it has both the lowest satisfaction rating and the lowest resolution rate.Prioritize improvements in Technical Support by reviewing unresolved cases, strengthening troubleshooting resources, and improving escalation processes. 

5.Call volume declined from 1,772 calls in January to 1,616 calls in February and remained nearly stable at 1,612 calls in March. The recommendation would be to Continue monitoring monthly call trends to determine whether the decline is seasonal or related to changes in customer demand. Staffing forecasts should be based on longer-term trends rather than only three months of data. 

6.Dan demonstrated the strongest overall performance, with the highest answer rate (82.62%), highest resolution rate (74.41%), and highest average customer satisfaction rating (2.85).Review Dan’s call-handling practices and use them as a benchmark for team coaching.

7.Diane had the lowest answer rate (79.15%), lowest resolution rate (71.41%), and lowest customer satisfaction score (2.70). However, Diane also had the fastest average speed of answer at approximately 273 seconds. 

## Recommendations Summary


Increase agent availability around 1:00 PM, the busiest call period.

Investigate the 18.92% unanswered calls by hour and topic.

Review unresolved Technical Support cases and improve technical resources.

Provide targeted coaching based on call-quality findings.

Use high-performing agent practices as internal benchmarks.

Monitor customer satisfaction by topic and agent.

Balance workloads, especially for agents handling higher call volumes.

Track call volume over a longer period to improve staffing forecasts.
